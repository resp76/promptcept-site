# Orbit SAT Math — Change Log

Newest first. Add an entry for every change to the `sat/` folder.

## 2026-09-28 — Grid-in questions, full adaptive section, spaced review, and new skills

Ideas came from reviewing [jsrinivasan1/SAT-practice](https://github.com/jsrinivasan1/SAT-practice) (grid-in questions, adaptive Module 2) and [open-spaced-repetition](https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/ts-fsrs) (spaced review). No code, questions, or libraries were copied or added. A search for a reusable SAT question bank found none: the College Board Question Bank's terms forbid reposting its questions, and community copies of it are © College Board. All new questions are generated by `tools/generate-questions.mjs`, with answers computed from each question's numbers.

**Question bank: 566 → 746**
- 120 grid-in questions (30 per category). The student types the answer instead of choosing one, as in about a quarter of real SAT Math questions.
- 60 new multiple-choice questions on five new skills:
  - Advanced Math: absolute value equations, and reading a parabola's graph (drawn on a coordinate grid)
  - Problem-Solving & Data: two-way tables (conditional probability), scatterplots with lines of best fit (predictions and residuals, drawn as a chart), and margin of error
- These skills also appear as grid-in questions.
- The original 566 questions are unchanged, so existing progress still lines up.

**Grid-in answers follow the digital SAT answer-box rules**
- Whole numbers, decimals, and fractions, such as 7/2 or 3.5, are accepted, as are equivalent fractions like 14/4.
- Up to 5 characters, or 6 with a minus sign. Mixed numbers like 3 1/2 are rejected with an explanation and don't count as an attempt.
- A repeating decimal must fill the box: .6666 or .6667 count for 2/3, but .67 doesn't, and Orbit explains why.
- An answer preview appears as you type. Typing a common wrong answer shows the same mistake-specific explanation as in multiple choice.

**Full Math section** (Practice → “Timed practice: module or full section”)
- 44 questions in two 35-minute modules, each with 22 questions (about 6 grid-in) in SAT domain proportions.
- Module 2 adapts: 14 or more right in Module 1 gives a harder Module 2 (mostly advanced questions); otherwise it's easier (mostly foundation and medium). This cutoff is Orbit's; College Board doesn't publish its own.
- Module 2 never repeats Module 1 questions. Results show the total, each module's score and version, domains, and a question-by-question review.
- Single timed modules are still available, and now include grid-in questions too.

**Spaced review of missed questions**
- A missed question comes back after 1 day, then 3, 7, and 21 days after each correct review. Four correct reviews in a row and it counts as mastered. Another miss starts the ladder again.
- Retrying early, before the review is due, doesn't move a question up the ladder.
- New “Spaced review” practice mode, a “due for spaced review” button on Today when questions are due, and a Spaced review panel and per-question schedule in the Mistake journal.
- The schedule is worked out from existing answer history, so it covers questions missed before this update.

**Wording fix for generated questions**
- When a randomly chosen number was 0, some questions showed it literally, for example “9x + 0 = 4x + 55” or “y = (x − 6)² + 0”. Zero terms are now left out (“9x = 4x + 55”), and “27 / (x + 0)” reads “27 / x”.
- Negative numbers after a minus sign are now written properly. Factors read “(x + 3)” instead of “(x − -3)”, and slope work reads “5 − (−3)” instead of “5 − -3”.
- Only wording changed in the affected existing questions (in prompts, hints, and explanations). Their answers, options, and IDs didn't change, so saved progress still matches.

**Other**
- Offline cache bumped to `orbit-sat-v7-gridin`.

## 2026-09-27 — Mistake feedback, timed practice modules, and geometry diagrams

These ideas came from reviewing three open-source projects. No code or questions were copied from any of them.
- [sugarforever/math-coach](https://github.com/sugarforever/math-coach) (MIT): its tutoring rules led to naming the rule behind every answer, pinpointing the exact mistake, and drawing the picture.
- [Anas099X/OpenSAT](https://github.com/Anas099X/OpenSAT): its practice-test generator led to the timed module. Its question database was not used. Some entries appear to be official College Board questions, and some AI-written ones are wrong.
- [Hemit99123/dailysat](https://github.com/Hemit99123/dailysat): nothing was taken. It has no license file and its seed questions are placeholders.

**Feedback on the specific mistake**
- All 400 generated questions now say why each wrong answer is tempting, for example: “You divided the whole $190 by $15 without first taking out the $40 sign-up fee.” It shows after a wrong answer, in the mistake journal, and in module reviews.
- Every generated question names the rule it uses, for example “Zero-product property: if (x − r)(x − s) = 0, then x = r or x = s.”
- The prompts, options, and answers of the 400 questions are unchanged, so existing progress still lines up.

**Timed practice module** (Practice → “Timed module: 22 questions, 35 min”)
- 22 questions in 35 minutes, the length of one digital SAT Math module.
- Questions follow the SAT domain split (8 Algebra, 8 Advanced Math, 3 Problem-Solving & Data, 3 Geometry & Trigonometry), prefer questions the learner hasn't seen, and get harder through the module.
- No hints during the test. Learners can skip, go back, mark questions for review, jump from a question grid, and use keys 1–4.
- The countdown keeps running if the learner leaves the page or reloads, and the module is scored automatically at 0:00.
- Results show the score, time, and a per-domain breakdown, plus a question-by-question review with the mistake note and rule. Every answer is saved to the learner's history and mistake journal. The last five modules can be reopened.
- Scores are shown as practice accuracy, not an estimated SAT score.

**Geometry diagrams**
- 76 geometry questions now include a labeled diagram: right triangles (Pythagorean theorem, missing leg, trigonometry with θ), triangle area with dashed height, triangle angles, rectangles, circles, and arc-length sectors.
- Diagrams are drawn from each question's own numbers, to scale where readable. Otherwise they carry “Note: figure not drawn to scale.”
- Each diagram has a screen-reader description.

**Other**
- Fixed the Practice sidebar showing the “Focus domain” and “Difficulty” labels above the wrong dropdowns.
- `questions-extra.js` is now minified, so it is 264 KB instead of 300 KB despite the new fields.
- Offline cache bumped to `orbit-sat-v6-modules`.

## 2026-09-27 — 400 new questions and multiple learners

**Question bank: 166 → 566 questions (+100 per category)**

| Category | Before | Added | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| Algebra | 51 | 100 | 151 |
| Advanced Math | 54 | 100 | 154 |
| Problem-Solving & Data | 5 | 100 | 105 |
| Geometry & Trigonometry | 56 | 100 | 156 |

- New questions live in `questions-extra.js`, generated by `tools/generate-questions.mjs`. Answers are calculated from each question's numbers, not typed by hand, and distractors are based on common mistakes.
- Each category uses 10–12 skill templates across foundation, medium, and advanced difficulty. Every question has a hint and a worked explanation.
- To change or add generated questions, edit the templates and run `node sat/tools/generate-questions.mjs`. The script refuses to write the file unless every category has exactly 100 questions, all prompts and IDs are unique, and every question has four distinct options.
- Original hand-written questions in `app.js` are unchanged.

**Multiple learners on one device**

- A "Who's studying?" screen lists every learner on the device. "Add a new learner" runs the normal onboarding.
- Each learner has separate progress, mistakes, goals, and study history.
- Optional 4–8 digit PIN per learner, set during onboarding or changed in Learner settings. It is stored as a SHA-256 hash, never as plain text.
- Learner settings now has "Switch learner". "Erase all study data" is now "Delete this learner" and only removes the signed-in learner.
- With a single learner and no PIN, the app still opens straight to that learner, as before.
- Existing progress from the single-learner version moves into the first learner profile automatically.
- Limit: profiles are per browser/device. Progress does not sync between devices, and the PIN keeps learners out of each other's profiles on a shared device but is not account security.

**Other**

- Offline cache bumped to `orbit-sat-v5-learners` and now includes `questions-extra.js`.
