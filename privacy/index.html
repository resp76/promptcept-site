✦ PromptCept

Privacy Policy

Effective July 28, 2026

Summary

PromptCept is designed to work without an account. Prompt names, task descriptions, clarifications, generated prompts, favorites, and settings are stored locally on your device.

Information we collect

Digital Sandbox Labs does not collect, track, sell, or transmit your PromptCept content. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

On-device prompt generation

PromptCept assembles prompts locally from built-in templates. Your content is not sent to Digital Sandbox Labs, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or xAI during prompt generation.

Third-party AI services

Content leaves PromptCept only when you intentionally copy it or ask PromptCept to open a third-party AI service. Anything you paste or submit there is governed by that service’s privacy policy and terms.

Local storage, backup, and deletion

PromptCept stores its library in app-local storage. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled. You may export a JSON backup; exported files are controlled by you and any storage provider you choose. Use Settings → Delete All Saved Prompts to erase the library, or uninstall the app to remove app-local data.

Children

PromptCept does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date and published text will be updated.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the public PromptCept support tracker.
